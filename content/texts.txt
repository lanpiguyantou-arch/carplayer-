#1 Where Are the Animals?
Colin: Look! There is a baby panda in the tree.|科林：看！树上有一只熊猫宝宝。
Alice: Where is its mother?|爱丽丝：它的妈妈在哪儿？
Colin: She was here just now. Now she is near the lake.|科林：它刚才还在这儿，现在在湖边。
Alice: Look at the ants! They were near the stone a moment ago.|爱丽丝：看那些蚂蚁！刚才它们还在石头旁边。
Colin: Now they are carrying some leaves.|科林：现在它们正在搬一些叶子。
Alice: Where are the butterflies?|爱丽丝：蝴蝶在哪儿呢？
Colin: They were on the flowers just now. Now they are over the water.|科林：它们刚才在花上，现在飞到水面上了。
Alice: Nature is so beautiful!|爱丽丝：大自然真美！
#2 My Hometown
Li Shan: Were you at home yesterday afternoon, Colin?|李山：科林，你昨天下午在家吗？
Colin: No, I wasn't. I was in my grandpa's village.|科林：不，我不在家。我在爷爷的村子里。
Li Shan: What was the village like?|李山：那个村子以前是什么样的？
Colin: Ten years ago, it was poor. There were only a few small houses.|科林：十年前它很穷，只有几间小房子。
Colin: There was no public square and no public toilet.|科林：那里没有公共广场，也没有公共卫生间。
Li Shan: What is it like now?|李山：现在呢？
Colin: Now there is a new bridge over the river.|科林：现在河上有一座新桥。
Colin: There are hotels, gardens and a big mall.|科林：还有宾馆、花园和一个大商场。
Li Shan: Your hometown is very different now!|李山：你的家乡现在大不一样了！
#3 My Weekend
Alice: Hi, Wu Chen. What did you do last weekend?|爱丽丝：嗨，吴晨。你上周末做了什么？
Wu Chen: On Saturday, I cleaned my room and washed my clothes.|吴晨：周六，我打扫了房间，洗了衣服。
Wu Chen: I also looked after my little sister.|吴晨：我还照顾了我的妹妹。
Alice: What did you do on Sunday?|爱丽丝：周日你做了什么？
Wu Chen: I went to the library and borrowed a book.|吴晨：我去图书馆借了一本书。
Wu Chen: I read it quietly in the reading room and took notes.|吴晨：我在阅览室安静地读书，还做了笔记。
Alice: Did you have a football match?|爱丽丝：你们举行足球比赛了吗？
Wu Chen: Yes, we did. We had a great time.|吴晨：是的，我们玩得很开心。
#4 My Holiday
Kitty: Where did you go on your holiday, Li Shan?|凯蒂：李山，你假期去了哪里？
Li Shan: I went to Qingdao with my parents.|李山：我和爸爸妈妈去了青岛。
Kitty: How did you go there?|凯蒂：你们怎么去的？
Li Shan: We went there by train. We booked the tickets early.|李山：我们坐火车去的，提前订了票。
Kitty: What did you do there?|凯蒂：你们在那儿做了什么？
Li Shan: We played beach volleyball and swam in the sea.|李山：我们打了沙滩排球，还在海里游泳。
Li Shan: We ate a lot of seafood. It was delicious!|李山：我们吃了很多海鲜，太好吃了！
Kitty: On National Day, I went to Shanghai Disneyland.|凯蒂：国庆节我去了上海迪士尼乐园。
Kitty: I rode the roller coaster and shook hands with cartoon characters.|凯蒂：我坐了过山车，还和卡通人物握了手。
#5 A Sports Meet
Liu Zhaoyang: Our school had a sports meet last Friday.|刘朝阳：上周五我们学校开了运动会。
Alice: What sport did you take part in?|爱丽丝：你参加了什么项目？
Liu Zhaoyang: I took part in the 100-meter race.|刘朝阳：我参加了100米赛跑。
Alice: Did you win?|爱丽丝：你赢了吗？
Liu Zhaoyang: No, I didn't. I fell down on the way.|刘朝阳：没有，我在途中摔倒了。
Liu Zhaoyang: But I got up and tried my best.|刘朝阳：但我站起来，尽了最大努力。
Alice: Good for you! I took part in the long jump.|爱丽丝：好样的！我参加了跳远。
Alice: Finally, our class won the high jump and the jump rope.|爱丽丝：最后，我们班赢了跳高和跳绳。
#6 The School Sale
Colin: Did you take part in the school sale yesterday?|科林：你昨天参加校园义卖了吗？
Amy: Yes, I did. I brought some magazines and notebooks.|艾米：参加了。我带了一些杂志和笔记本。
Colin: What did you sell?|科林：你卖了什么？
Amy: I sold all of them! I also sold some popcorn.|艾米：全卖完了！我还卖了一些爆米花。
Colin: What did you do with the money?|科林：你们用这些钱做什么了？
Amy: We gave it to a Hope School.|艾米：我们把钱捐给了一所希望小学。
Colin: Then we watched a funny show and laughed a lot.|科林：然后我们看了一场有趣的表演，笑个不停。
Amy: We had a good time!|艾米：我们玩得很开心！
#7 At the Restaurant
Waiter: Good evening! Here is the menu.|服务员：晚上好！这是菜单。
Dad: Thank you. We'd like a plate of beef and a bowl of rice.|爸爸：谢谢。我们要一盘牛肉和一碗米饭。
Waiter: Would you like something to drink?|服务员：要喝点什么吗？
Dad: Yes, please. A cup of tea for me.|爸爸：好的，我要一杯茶。
Waiter: What would you like, juice or water?|服务员：你想要果汁还是水？
Kitty: I'd like a glass of juice with ice, please.|凯蒂：请给我一杯加冰的果汁。
Kitty: Can I have a piece of pizza too?|凯蒂：我还能要一块披萨吗？
Waiter: Sure. Anything else?|服务员：当然。还要别的吗？
Dad: No, thanks. That's all.|爸爸：不用了，谢谢。就这些。
#8 New Year Is Coming
Alice: New Year is coming! What's the date today?|爱丽丝：新年要到了！今天几号？
Li Shan: It's December 30th.|李山：今天是12月30日。
Alice: What did you do this year?|爱丽丝：你今年做了什么？
Li Shan: I read some books about science and learned to swim.|李山：我读了一些科学书，还学会了游泳。
Alice: Do you have a plan for the new year?|爱丽丝：你有新年计划吗？
Li Shan: Yes, I do. I wish to join our ping-pong team.|李山：有。我希望加入我们的乒乓球队。
Li Shan: At the Spring Festival, we put up couplets and hang lanterns.|李山：春节时，我们贴对联、挂灯笼。
Li Shan: We visit relatives and friends and exchange greetings.|李山：我们走亲访友，互相问候。
Alice: Happy New Year!|爱丽丝：新年快乐！
